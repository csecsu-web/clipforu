My Clipper

Choose a video

This app runs in your GitHub Codespace. Your video is uploaded to that Codespace while you work on it. Delete the source when you finish. Unfinished jobs are removed after 6 hours; exported clips stay in the Codespace until you download them or delete them.

30-minute videos are supported. Processing time depends on the Codespace CPU. The first run downloads the speech model and can take longer. The app checks available storage before saving an upload.
